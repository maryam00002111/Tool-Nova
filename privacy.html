Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

How our tools handle your data

Most tools on ToolNova — including text, developer, color, calculator and image tools — process everything locally in your browser using JavaScript. Files and text you enter into these tools are not uploaded to our servers.

Information we collect

We collect standard, aggregated analytics (pages visited, approximate location, device type) to understand how the site is used. If you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us, we store the email address or details you provide for that purpose only.

Cookies and advertising

ToolNova may show ads through third-party ad networks, which can use cookies to serve relevant advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Third-party libraries

Some tools (like QR code and PDF generation) load small open-source JavaScript libraries from a public CDN to work. These libraries run in your browser and do not send your tool input anywhere.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our contact page.
